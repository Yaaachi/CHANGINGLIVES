WHO WE ARE

Practical compassion. Shared dignity. Lasting relationships.

Changing Lives Foundation works at the meeting point of community development, practical assistance and Christian ministry. Our purpose is to stand alongside people through everyday challenges and significant moments of need, while creating spaces for belonging, encouragement and participation.

Our activities reach across generations: from children and young people taking part in sport and community gatherings, to older residents being celebrated through hospitality and companionship. Alongside these efforts, we bring people together through public prayer, worship, creative expression and community health activities.

OUR FOCUS

Where compassion becomes action

Our programmes respond to practical, social and spiritual needs.

Food and essential support

Food distribution and practical assistance can ease immediate pressure on families experiencing hardship. Our community-centred approach brings volunteers and local supporters together to respond with dignity and care.

Older people and dignity

We create occasions for older residents to gather, receive gifts, enjoy companionship and be recognised. These gatherings value the experience of senior citizens and encourage intergenerational connection.

Housing and household wellbeing

Our documented activities include housing-related support, repairs and household improvements. Safe and dignified living conditions are a foundation for stronger families and communities.

Youth, sport and development

Sport, organised gatherings and positive community engagement help young people build teamwork, discipline and confidence. Our activities also carry messages opposing abuse and vandalism.

Prayer and spiritual encouragement

Our Christian identity informs public prayer, fellowship and support during challenging times. We bring communities together for shared reflection, encouragement and spiritual care.

Arts, culture and healthy living

Through worship gatherings and artist participation, we create opportunities for local and established talent to connect with audiences. Community walks and marathons also promote participation in healthy living.

A vision rooted in faith, expressed through service

Changing Lives Foundation, also known as Changing Lives Touching Nations, is a South African community organisation whose Christian ministry identity is reflected in both practical service and collective prayer. Led by founder and visionary Sis Buli, the organisation connects volunteers, faith leaders, community members and supporters around needs that affect everyday life. Its work includes food support, gatherings for older people, youth engagement, housing-related assistance, health activities and opportunities for musical talent. We believe meaningful service begins by listening to communities, responding respectfully and building relationships that endure beyond a single event.
